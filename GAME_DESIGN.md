# Game design (living document)

Claude keeps this up to date after every change. It's the game's memory: what exists, what friends
asked for, and what nobody has decided yet.

## Fixed

- Multiplayer: one friend hosts, others join with a code.
- Voice chat.
- 3D.
- The shared goal (#75): **free the Commune from your landlord and get into heaven**, over five
  chapters that use every mechanic, ending in the landlord's eviction (#11) and the gates of
  heaven opening.

## Gameplay loop

<!-- The current loop in plain words, for friends. Refreshed on every change; the Discord bot
posts this section every few landed ideas (#153). Keep it under ~1800 characters. -->
**Play in the browser:** https://cumsauce.github.io/friendSLOP/

**Same Wi-Fi?** Host, and friends on your network see a "Join <you>'s commune" button.

**The goal:** free the Commune from your landlord and get into heaven, over 5 chapters.

**Each chapter:** a checklist (top-left) of jobs in new places:
1 *Moving In*: leash the strays (picnic corner), eat the mushroom, climb the sky islands to Abuela in heaven.
2 *Rent Day*: open a vault door, break out of HALT, hide and seek, grab burgers at Wendy's.
3 *The Heist*: carry out the Fire totem, use gods tears, a duel, trade a crow egg (crows: by the windmill) to Sewer
Paul in the tunnels. 4 *General Strike*: all of it at once, plus 2 soul pieces from the
warehouse for Jegg. 5 *The Eviction*: make Jegg whole (5 pieces) and the rest, one last time.
Each chapter ends with the landlord: pay rent in organs, pickpocket him, then a boss fight.
Each ticked job is a Heaven Pass tier; chapter 5 opens heaven. The host's game saves the
chapter and its ticks, so hosting again (even after an update) carries on where you left off.

**Always going on:** the Stillness freezes everything for 75 s every 16 minutes. Fart near an
NPC and get caught 4 times: villagers chase you and float you. Villagers fart and blame you.
Sprinting and climbing burn stamina (climbing much faster): run dry and you can't sprint until
you've caught your breath, and a spent climber drops off the wall. The stamina card (bottom right)
only shows while it's being spent or coming back. Low morality brings a helicopter. 5% of actions fail sillily. Marmalade's wheel bends a rule. Your fat cat grows unwatched and drinks pee; at 10x
it gobbles up you or anyone in its mouth.
Say "mute" at a friend (Q) and they're silenced until they RPG you. Stand still 3 min
and systemd (PID 1) kills you; wade out of your depth in the lake for 12 s and you drown. Tiny notes under the Cat card (bottom right) say which world-wide effect is on right now. Say hi (T), or high five, dap up or rock paper scissors a friend (U, O). Pointless achievements pop up for walking, spinning and standing around.
**Dying** puts you in heaven: friends beg at your grave (out loud counts double) within 60 s,
or read the Tome of Uncancelling over it. Otherwise it's hell: hop across a lava pit to get out.
**Day and night** (#284): a 24-minute day everyone shares; the sun crosses the sky, dusk goes
golden, nights are dark blue with stars, a moon, and a city whose windows and street lamps light up. Some nights you hear whispers behind you.
Every way in or out (doors, stairs, manholes, train doors) glows and names where it goes.
**In New York:** find the J to Brooklyn in Fulton St station, 40 levels down (past empty
long subway platforms, concourses of looping corridors and long curved galleries, rooms two floors high, and train-tunnel rings; most levels have 2-3 stairs down and signs to the nearest), for Pelf, while
Andrew calls, gets angry, and sends something after you that crawls the ducts between staff doors (hide in lockers). Everyone carries
$100; going down may drop your wallet somewhere in the station: find it (and strangers'
wallets), or press I for an insurance drone that keeps 70%. B brags: coins fall out for others. Or step into the
brownstone with the neon SURF sign: a surf map, a 3.7 km seven-stage course of bending ramps between sky towers to a timed finish.
The streets and stations have tweakers screaming at nobody, preachers telling you you're
going to hell, and bucket drummers (thickest on the PATH platform and round Fulton St).
Step in front of a cab and you're punted and ragdoll for a couple of seconds before getting up.
**Horror ARG:** a faceless Watcher stands far behind one of you; look at it and it vanishes
in static, leaving a scrap of a message. Find all 5, then gather in the ruins' tower: a buried
TV broadcasts a riddle that leads outside the game.

**Far north:** climb over the rim hills and the world goes on: empty wasteland, dunes, mesas and
dry washes, the same for everyone, up to 4 km out. Nothing lives there yet.

**Far south:** a PATH train waits at a little station near the valley's south edge. Step in
and it pulls out along a stone viaduct, rattles along in the dark (a bored robot voice over the
loudspeaker calling the stop) and comes out of a tunnel in a hall under the Oculus in New York City; climb the stairs
up into the Oculus. The city has One World Trade Center, 4 shops to walk into, 3 boba shops (Gong Chaw,
Kung Fu Tee, Tiger Sugah) where your dollars buy drinks that stack a speed buzz, and the Vessel on a
plaza west of the bodega: a copper honeycomb of stairs you can climb 18 levels to a ring walkway 42 m up — where a
glowing knob dares you to jump off: click it and you ragdoll the whole way down to a blood
splat on the plaza before getting up. The train under the Oculus rides you back. At JFK, a gate at the city's north end, Flight 305 takes off
for **Miami**, an island floating high over the city: walk into Factory Town for Miami Music
Week (EDM, lasers, a dancing crowd) and buy a bump of ketamine from the guy by the bar.
**Penn Station** is the Garden's drum on 7th Ave (or the 1 train from beside JFK): Rose Pizza,
a bathroom line where a stranger talks nonsense then leaves you 15 s of silence, a cold NJ
Transit side (chained gates, trash fires, stains, ominous music) and a warm LIRR side (calm tune).
WTC One's elevator rides to **High Places**, a rooftop dispensary 428 m up: buy a joint ($20) and
light up for a draw-timing minigame (keep it lit, don't cough, waste nothing). Its soft serve
machine is OUT OF ORDER until someone finds its three parts in the city's shops (shared quest).
The bodega sells **statement hats** everyone can see. Pigeon flocks wheel overhead and poop on you;
now and then they all poop at once.
Gangs of baby kittens prowl the avenue: get close and they charge, meowing, and swat your
ankles until they knock you flying. Run: they're slower than you.

**Just for fun:** Mei, smoking (joints stack a wobbly, colour-shifting high), lex pile, the bear (north forest), the goat (pond), Discord dates, rat twins, saying hi (T), toilets of feces that pop up near you (eat for 20 s of double speed), the Lily Garden by the pond (carry love letters between two shy women until they get together). Farts (1 in 20) and feces pay KoriCoins™, spent on claw machines (by the gambling table, in every boba shop and on every subway platform) where you steer a real claw over a pit of tumbling figures and drop it, for little figures you keep between games (main menu: Kori prizes) and can hold in your hand (the . key) for a little speed.

**Where it's thin:** what you carry back only ticks the checklist; organs only come from the
chapter refill and the landlord. Spells, stats and being shot don't change play. Gambling is
free, and organ dollars only buy boba, joints and hats so far (and Wendy's butcher turns spare organs into dollars). Six players beat the landlord fast. Only the RPG, a 10x cat, systemd and the lake can kill you. The wastes are empty.

## What exists

- A grassy meadow floor (see "Nicer look" below).
- **A map (M).** A live top-down view of the area you're in, painted like a paper map: ink lines,
  flat colours, a burnt edge, a pin on each player and the area's place names. Indoors the
  ceiling is cut away. `crates/game/src/ui/map.rs`, `data/game/map.ron`, `shaders/paper_map.wesl`.
- **Mic is a setting.** There's no mute key any more: "Microphone" and "Push to talk (V)" sit in
  settings. The first-person / overview camera moved from V to C (C copies the invite only while
  the journal is open).
- **Ways in and out are marked.** Every entrance, exit, stair, ladder and hatch gets one glowing
  threshold marker with a label within 10 m (mint in, butter out, lilac up/down); markers only
  show the way. `crates/play/src/features/doorway.rs`, `data/play/doorway.ron`.
- **Structure pipeline.** Buildings come from a shared kit (`crates/structures`) and a world seed
  the host rolls each session (`features/world_seed.rs`; `FRIENDSLOP_SEED=n` fixes it), so all
  peers build the same world: generated village houses on six plots, seeded furniture, a seeded
  sewer room graph, and a warehouse plan from `crates/wfc`. Open: furniture in the sewer and
  warehouse; more house types.
- **Lived-in after the storm.** The commune map dresses the village backyards and the camp's
  edge with laundry lines, raised vegetable beds and storm repairs in progress (a sawhorse,
  tarped planks, a wheelbarrow, a rain barrel): `cv-*` modeler prefabs placed on `commune-map`,
  visual only (no colliders), kept off the villagers' streets.
- **First-person bodies** (#53, #317). Everyone walks in first person (WASD, mouse, Space jump,
  Shift run); left click uses what's under the crosshair; V swaps to the overview camera; Esc
  opens the pause screen. The host moves bodies with a kinematic controller (slopes, stairs,
  jumps) that can't pass trees, rocks or walls. `friendslop-base::kcc`, `data/play/fps.ron`.
- **Abuela in heaven** (#105). The highest sky island is a small heaven where Abuela knits in a
  rocking chair; click her and she says loving Spanish phrases (with English gloss) in a
  synthesised old voice. `features/abuela.rs`.
- **Climbing and floating islands** (#99). Hold W and Space against anything steep to climb it;
  three grassy islands float above the south-east meadow, reached by climbing rock spires.
  Nothing is up there yet but the view.
- **Stamina.** Sprinting and climbing spend a shared stamina bar (climbing costs about twice as
  much a second), shown as a card in the bottom-right stack only while it's being spent or
  recovering. At zero you're exhausted: no sprinting or climbing until it's back to 30%, and a
  climber lets go and falls. It comes back only with your feet on the ground.
  `crates/play/src/features/stamina.rs`, `data/play/stamina.ron`.
- **Make your own character** (#100). The menu's Character button opens a creator with a live
  3D preview (body type, size, colours, eyes, ears, horns, tail, accessory); it's saved locally,
  sent to the host and seen by everyone. Bodies are procedural rigs shared with villagers.
  `people.rs`.
- **Hide and seek** (#61). Press K (2+ players): a random seeker is blindfolded 30 s, then has
  3 min to tag hiders, who become seekers; ends when nobody hides or time runs out, then loops.
  Name labels are hidden during rounds and hiders hear a heartbeat when a seeker is near.
- **Districts across the wider map.** Cover is spread over a ±90 m map: north forest, south-west
  ruins with a hedge maze and quarry, west valley, meadows with a windmill, plus fine dressing
  (reeds, scarecrows, lanterns). Counts in `data/game/scenery.ron`; `zones.rs`.
- **Commune villagers** (#61). Twenty villagers built like players wander between hangouts
  (host-simulated, navmesh overland trips), blend in for hide and seek, and say hello when
  clicked.
- **The Discord friends walk the commune** (#159). Each friend who sent an idea is a villager;
  chat one up for heart-and-line replies, and on the 5th chat they are dating you. Names in
  `villagers::NAMES` and `discord_friends::LINES`. Dating does nothing else yet.
- **Evil rats in the tunnels** (#159). Each friend has a backwards-named evil rat twin in the
  tunnels; click one to adopt it, and it follows you and says evil lines when clicked.
- **One click, one thing.** A click picks the single closest clickable thing within about
  3.5 m; the server re-checks and refusals show as a toast. `features/interact.rs`.
- **Ambient life.** Local butterflies, pollen, fireflies, leaves, birds, campfire smoke and
  swaying props, about 190 recycled particles. `ambience.rs`.
- **Lots to hide behind** (#60). About 44 seeded clusters of groves, rocks, hedges, logs, walls,
  sheds, bales and crates with static colliders; landmarks stay clear.
- **A bigger world.** The walkable area is ±90 m with a soft wall and hills beyond; flat commune
  and village, a rolling forest, a climbable hill, lake and quarry pit, with dirt paths to every
  district. `crates/world/src/terrain.rs`.
- **Nature, campfire and generated music** (#62, #127, #235, #324). The floor has low-poly
  trees, bushes, a campfire, pond with ducks and lantern-lined paths (`crates/world/src/landmarks.rs`).
  A generated, adaptive 90s jazz-fusion soundtrack picks songs by mood and place and shifts key
  as you climb (`features/fusion`, own crate `crates/fusion`, data in `assets/data/music/`).
  Plus sound effects, an ambient bed, and rare Night whispers behind you at night
  (`features/whispers.rs`).
- **3D sound** (`spatial.rs`). World sounds are heard where they are (distance, panning, muffling
  behind colliders). On headphones the loudest few sounds (12, `data/base/spatial.ron`) also get a
  head-related filter (`friendslop-hrtf`), so you can hear a sound above, below and behind you;
  Settings has a "3D sound (headphones)" switch. Voice chat is proximity-based but never fully silent. CC0 assets come from
  Git LFS and fall back to procedural looks if missing (`crates/game/assets/CREDITS.md`).
- **Currency: organ dollars** (#22, #135, #217). One money: organ dollars (`$`). An organ is worth
  its organ dollars (creature worth × organ importance); players hold an organ purse, refilled each
  chapter, that pays the landlord's rent. Wendy's butcher buys your cheapest organ for exactly its
  value in organ dollars at the chopping block. `features/organ_butcher.rs`,
  `data/side/organ_butcher.ron`.
- **Crows** (#2). Three black crows hop around and caw with a sound; server spawns them.
- **The bear** (#4). One cute brown bear ambles about and growls every ~13 s. Nothing else yet.
- **Voice-altering mushroom** (#6). Click the mushroom to eat it and your voice pitch-shifts
  higher or lower for 45 s; a new one grows 10 s later.
- **Cat-vision palette** (#12). All materials go through a cat-vision filter (blues and
  yellows, no red-green) in a bright storybook look.
- **Day/night cycle** (#284). A 24-minute day runs off the real clock, so everyone sees the same
  sun, moon, stars and sky; the city lights up at night. Dark places set their own look through
  `place.rs`. `atmosphere.rs`, `night_lights.rs`, `data/game/day_night.ron`.
- **Nicer look** (#47). All art is generated in code (`art.rs`): procedural grass, warm sun
  with soft shadows, haze, and extra detail on creatures and props.
- **PBR surfaces on the primitives** (#128). Plain materials get generated stone, wood, leaves,
  cloth, metal or painted textures and normal maps, sized in metres. `crates/world/src/surfaces.rs`.
- **Duels** (#14). Challenge a player with a number key; they get bodies in a walled arena,
  one bullet, every hit kills, three lives each; last one standing wins. Bragging rights only.
- **Commune of Dave** (#3, #43, #57). Parliament is a smouldering ruin with an anarchist
  commune flag, Dave and a banner. Scenery only.
- **The judgmental goat** (#20). A goat comments passive-aggressively on joins, leaves, the
  mushroom, muting and doing nothing.
- **Morality** (#21). Every player has a morality score (bottom-left); harming landlords and
  the rich is always rewarded. It carries over between chapters. Bad scores bring the
  helicopter.
- **The helicopter** (#134). At morality -6 or lower a helicopter hunts you and fires bursts of
  bullets; it leaves when you recover. Being shot only shows a message so far.
- **Farting** (#18). Gas builds over 40 s; press F to fart. If an NPC is within 4 m you are
  shamed for 20 s (blur, alarm), and everyone sees who farted.
- **Fart noises** (#26). Every fart is synthesised by a physical lips-and-cheek model; each
  player tunes pitch, length and timbre with `[ ] - = ; '`.
- **Romance** (#97). One shameful fart in three, the NPC who caught you falls for you with
  hearts and an "aww" chorus. It's only a moment.
- **Punishment** (#123). Four catches trigger 60 s of attack mode, where all villagers chase
  you to frantic music.
- **The shipwreck** (#185). The beached *Rainbow Reckoning* on the lake shore has three pirates
  guarding a chest; beat them in melee to open it for 25 doubloons, which the bodega takes.
  `features/shipwreck.rs`; first feature to claim ground via `world::footprints`.
- **Click hints.** The aim point shows a label saying what clicking does, or "too far".
  `features/interact.rs`.
- **Skills** (#176). Six skills (walking, farting, sleeping, pissing, smoking, dueling) level
  1-100 by doing them, some with bonuses. `features/skills.rs`.

- **The Watcher** (#277, Horror ARG 1/3 of #182). Now and then a tall faceless figure appears far
  behind one player; it vanishes in a burst of static when anyone looks at it or gets close.
  `data/side/watcher.ron` (`features/watcher.rs`).
- **The Watcher's scraps** (#278, Horror ARG 2/3). Where it vanishes it leaves a scrap of paper;
  click to read the next piece of a shared coded message that points at the ruins' tower.
  `data/text/clues.ron`, `data/side/clues.ron` (`features/clues.rs`).
- **The broadcast** (#279, Horror ARG 3/3). Once all scraps are found, gather near the half-buried
  TV in the ruins and it plays creepy lines, the last one in ROT13 pointing at `SIGNAL.txt` in
  the repo. `data/text/broadcast.ron`, `data/side/broadcast.ron` (`features/broadcast.rs`).
- **New York City** (#283). Past the south rim is a city with an avenue, cross streets, cabs,
  walk-ups and towers, street furniture and parked cars, a park, One World Trade Center and the
  Oculus, and four enterable shops (bodega, pizza, laundromat, diner). Getting there is by the
  PATH train. `data/side/nyc.ron`, `rules/nyc.rs` (`features/nyc.rs`); see-through glass in
  `data/base/glass.ron`.
- **The Vessel** (part of #283). A full-size climbable Vessel with 154 flights you can walk to the
  top; a knob on top lets you jump and ragdoll to the plaza. `features/vessel.rs`,
  `features/vessel_jump.rs`, `data/side/vessel_jump.ron`.
- **Boba shops** (#356). Three parody boba shops with staff and bubbly music; buy drinks with
  dollars for stacking timed speed boosts and screen tints. `data/side/boba.ron`,
  `rules/boba.rs` (`features/boba.rs`).
- **Trains** (#321). Stand in a waiting car and the train leaves the station, rides through a
  dark tunnel while a retro voice announces the stop, then arrives at the other station. Lines
  are data, so more trains can be added. `data/side/trains.ron` (`features/trains.rs`).
- **Penn Station** (#357, #352). Reached by the 1 train or the doors of a Penn pavilion: Rose
  Pizza, a restroom with a weird stranger, 15 s of silence and a filthy mirror, a grim NJ
  Transit wing and a clean LIRR wing, each with its own music. Layout is map markers.
  Both wings were doubled: NJT gets more chained shops, a dead escalator "out of service
  since 2009", a tipped vending machine, a leak bucket, a mattress and a fourth trash fire;
  past the LIRR waiting room runs a 100 m hall with a 10 m wood-beamed ceiling, ten open shops
  a side (signs, counters, shelves of stock), a hanging flag and clock, glass doors to the
  tracks at the end, and commuters walking up and down it (count and pace in penn.ron).
  `data/side/penn.ron`, `penn.map.json` (`features/penn.rs`).
- **Pigeons** (#355). Boid flocks around the city and inside buildings; they poop, and
  occasionally all at once, with a property damage bill. Local visuals.
  `data/side/pigeons.ron`.
- **Kitten gangs** (#374). Gangs of baby kittens roam New York streets, charge you when near and
  swat you until you ragdoll. `data/side/kittens.ron`.
- **High Places** (#349). A rooftop dispensary on One World Trade: buy a joint and smoke it in a
  draw-and-cool minigame. `data/side/high_places.ron`.
- **High Places' soft serve** (#350). A broken ice cream machine; find three parts in three shops
  (shared progress) to fix it for free cones. `data/side/ice_cream.ron`.
- **Hats** (#323). Buy statement hats in the bodega; everyone sees what you wear.
  `data/side/hats.ron`.
- **Doubloons at the bodega** (#215). The shop takes doubloons to cover any dollar shortfall.
  `doubloon_dollars` in `hats.ron`.
- **Street people** (#358). Fifteen tweakers, preachers and performers around the city, most in
  Penn Station, the PATH platform and Fulton St. Local dressing. `data/side/street_people.ron`.
- **Miami Music Week** (#345). Fly from JFK to a Miami island with the Factory Town club: EDM
  music, dancers, and a dealer for a short ketamine trip (only you feel it). Planes are train
  lines. `data/side/miami.ron` (`features/miami.rs`).
- **Surf map** (#306). A brownstone's neon-signed door leads to a sky surf course with five
  stages, checkpoints, strong air control and a best-time timer. Inside the hall you move like a Counter-Strike surf server (Source units at 0.0254 m: sv_airaccelerate 150, the 30 u/s air wish cap, 800 u/s² gravity, accelerate 10, friction 4, stopspeed 75, 250 u/s wish speed, 3500 u/s top speed): faces steeper than 45° aren't ground, so you slide along them with your velocity clipped onto the face and no friction, and strafing while you turn adds speed (`kcc::Air`, ahoy's PM_AirAccelerate). A generated city crowds the ramps: towers rising past them on both sides, cranes, girders overhead, and building sites under the ramps with kickers to launch back on. Each stage has its own neon colour (pad edge strips, a light, a marker post with one block per stage number, lane lights floating beside the ramps) and a gold arch stands over the finish (`data/side/surf_dressing.ron`, no colliders). `data/side/surf.ron`,
  `rules/surf.rs` (`features/surf.rs`). Big set pieces (doomy) are placed around the course from the city and the ramps: neon arches, truss portals and floating rings over the ramps, a FINISH gantry, rooftop water towers, billboards, helipads, masts, pools and tower cranes, sky bridges between towers, and blimps, hot-air balloons, giant arrows, disco balls and floating rest platforms beside it (`data/side/surf_props.ron`, `features/surf_props.rs`). The sky follows the camera up there, so day and night look right. The pillar run: stage 3's long curving ramp is planted with neon pillars in its faces, left then right, to weave between (`data/side/surf_pillars.ron`, `features/surf_pillars.rs`). Three more stretches (`data/side/surf_runs.ron`, `features/surf_runs.rs`): a fin chicane (neon walls filling one whole face, left then right, on stage 5's first ramp), an orb garden (floating glowing orbs to weave between, on stage 1's first ramp) and a gate run (neon frames to surf through, on stage 3's last ramp). Past the old finish there's a sixth stage: the office tower (`data/side/surf_office.ron`, `features/surf_office.rs`, doomy: "just a snake back and forth", so it was redone as three lines): the main line runs in through a huge glass wall, sweeps south round a glass elevator core, cruises over an indoor garden with a pool, crosses over the shortcut and plunges round the north bend; the floor cut is a 22 m leap off the main line onto a straight, faster ramp that runs under it through the boardroom gates and a speed gap; the skybridge chord is a short leap off the north bend onto its last stretch. Tiers of glass executive offices line both long walls and skybridges cross high overhead. The old finish is now a stage-7 pad leading on into the mall (`data/side/surf_mall.ron`, `features/surf_mall.rs`): one ramp winding in two big S-bends through a huge glass-roofed mall, with tiers of storefronts, balconies and neon signs up both walls, a food court on the floor far below, FOOD COURT signs, and catwalks across the hall to go over, under or duck, then the finish. The course now ends in the foundry (`data/side/surf_foundry.ron`, `features/surf_foundry.rs`, stages 14-16, brief in `docs/design/surf-areas-brief.md`): a molten complex of brick furnace stacks, cooling towers, crane gantries, conveyors, turning gears, bobbing pistons and lava falls over a lava sea, with glowing orange ramps. Every stage splits into a wide main line and a shorter shortcut with a jump and pillars to dodge, both landing on the next pad: a bend, a full-turn spiral round the Great Furnace (the shortcut is a tighter inner spiral that skips about a third of it), and a calm cooling-tower alley then a fast finish run under a gold FINISH arch. The overgrown ruins (stages 10-12, `data/side/surf_overgrown.ron`, `features/surf_overgrown.rs`) are a jungle-reclaimed city on three terraces with waterfalls: a collapsed highway interchange (a fork: easy two-hop chain or a 20 m median leap), a half-fallen skyscraper you helix down round (1 turn, a cross-over), and a drowned mall with a calm flat cruise under a banyan and a second fork (skylight leap).
- **Ragdolling** (#320). A cab hit punts you and you tumble as a physics body for a moment before
  getting up. `data/play/ragdoll.ron` (`crates/play/src/features/ragdoll.rs`).
- **Fulton St station** (no issue number). A subway entrance leads down a 40-level maze of
  generated corridors; follow the J Brooklyn signs to the J train for +3 Pelf once. Needs ground
  holes (`friendslop_world::holes`). `data/side/fulton.ron`, `fulton_tiles.ron`
  (`features/fulton.rs`).
- **Fulton St level types** (doomy: set pieces, not a tileset jumble). Each level is planned
  coarse first: it gets a type (Warren: corridors and small rooms; Hall: one big tall hall
  with columns; Atrium: a hall whose floor drops a whole level, balconies at its doors, long
  stairs down, the level below solid under it; Line: every platform level, one long straight
  track and platform along its long edge, 48 m) and a footprint that fits it (6x6 up to
  12x6), centred under the station. A small WFC over 12 m macro cells places the set piece,
  then the usual WFC runs corridors up to it. Stairs always come down in the middle 6x6, so
  levels of any size line up; tunnel levels and the two above them stay 6x6 Warren, and no
  atrium or tall hall sits over a platform level. When a set piece won't fit, that level is
  plain corridors. Weights, depths, sizes in `data/side/fulton_layout.ron`; the big halls'
  dressing is `rooms.kinds` with `set_piece: true`. Not yet tried in game (hunter in big
  halls, frame time on a Line level).
- **Fulton St concourses** (doomy: more ways round, long curved halls, not a maze). A
  fifth level type, Concourse (10x10 up to 12x10), is planned as a graph, not by WFC
  (`features/fulton/graph.rs`): stairs and landings first, then 1-2 galleries (12 m wide halls
  of straights and rounded quarter turns, columns down the middle) and 1-3 rooms (some
  L-shaped), then corridors routed between every doorway (wandering a little, few turns),
  1-3 extra corridors that close loops, and short dead ends (where the lockers are). If it
  doesn't fit, the level is plain corridors at the same size. Tuning in
  `fulton_layout.ron` (`concourse`).
- **Two-storey rooms** (doomy: a room two levels high with a balcony). A concourse over another
  concourse may (`two_storey` chance) drop its biggest room's floor a whole level: balconies at
  its doors, long stairs down, and its floor is the level below's floor. The level below runs
