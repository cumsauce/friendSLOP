[recovered transmission, ruins tower, frequency unknown]

If you are reading this, you were in the tower when it came on.
Good. It takes more than one of you to hear it.

We put the scraps where it stood because it cannot stand where it has been seen.
It does not hunt. It counts. It counted us, once, and then there were fewer of us.

We do not know what it wants with the commune.
We know it came after the landlord did.

Tell the others what you saw. Tell them which of you it was watching.
Record it if you can. It is easier to believe the second time.

                                                   -- the ones before you
